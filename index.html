📦 Guide

How to get your Spotify Data ZIP

Spotify allows you to download a complete export of your personal listening history. Follow these quick steps to request and download it:

1

Open Privacy Settings

Go to your Spotify Privacy Settings ↗ in your browser.

2

Find "Download your data"

Scroll down to the Download your data section on the page.

3

Request Data

Select Account data (or Extended streaming history) and click Request data.

4

Download & Drop

Spotify will email you when it's ready. Download the .zip and drop it above!

🔒 100% Client-Side Privacy: Your file is read and processed entirely inside your browser. No data ever leaves your computer or gets uploaded to any server.
